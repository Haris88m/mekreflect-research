# Source attribution and reuse

Epoch AI data comes from https://epoch.ai/data/benchmark_data.zip and its documented use conditions at https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/use-this-data. The included Epoch-administered data is attributed to Epoch AI under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. It has been transformed into JSON, joined by source model identifiers and filtered to the explicitly included tables. Archive hashes, timestamps and source settings are retained in the data module. External-administered tables with separate rights were excluded.

arXiv metadata is retrieved through the arXiv API. See https://info.arxiv.org/help/api/tou.html. Metadata is available under CC0; individual papers retain their own licenses. This repository does not redistribute paper full text or imply that a preprint is independently validated.

Regional and policy notes link to their official European Commission, Eurostat and JRC sources. Those links and bounded descriptions do not imply that MEKreflect produced the underlying official statistics.
